    How it works

    From empty calendar to scanned ticket.

    Set it up once. Every day after that runs itself.

    1. Set your hours

      Pick your dates, opening times, slot length and how many guests each slot holds. Price by day, by slot or by ticket type.

    2. Connect Square

      Sign in with your Square account. Ticket money lands in your account on Square's normal schedule. We never hold your funds.

    3. Make it yours

      Your logo, colors and domain on every page guests see. Confirmation emails come from your name and your address.

    4. Scan at the gate

      Use the TicketBloom scanner on iPhone or Android. Scan one ticket in a group and check in the whole party with one tap.

    What you get

    Made for days that sell out by the half hour.

    Capacity per slot

    Each entry time has its own limit, so 11:00 never gets 600 people while 3:30 sits empty. Sold-out slots close on their own.

    Your brand, start to finish

    White-label by default. Guests buy on your domain, get email from you, and never have to learn our name.

    Paid to your Square

    Payments run on your own Square account, alongside the card readers you already use at the entrance and the gift shop.

    Wallet passes

    Every ticket can go straight into Apple Wallet, so guests aren't digging through email in the parking lot.

    A faster gate

    Scan, see the party size, wave them through. Staff can also look up an order by name when a guest can't find their ticket.

    Know what sold it

    See which ad, email or post brought in each order, so next year's budget goes where the tickets came from.
