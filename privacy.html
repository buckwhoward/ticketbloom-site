Privacy Policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

TicketBloom (“TicketBloom”, “we”, “us”) is ticketing software for timed-entry attractions, based in Utah. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. It covers this website (getticketbloom.com), the TicketBloom platform used by attraction operators and their staff, and the TicketBloom Scanner app for iPhone and Android.

The short version

  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not use ticket buyers' information for our own marketing.
  • When you buy tickets from an attraction that uses TicketBloom, the attraction is in charge of your information. We handle it for them, under their instructions.
  • Card payments are processed by Square. We never receive or store full card numbers.
  • Each attraction's data is kept separate from every other attraction's data.
  • The Scanner app uses the camera only to read ticket codes. It does not record audio, track location, or contain advertising or analytics software.

1. Our two roles

For attraction operators and our website. When an attraction operator (an “organizer”) signs up, when its staff use TicketBloom, or when you visit this website or ask us for a demo, we decide how that information is used. This policy applies directly.

For ticket buyers and guests. Organizers sell tickets on their own branded storefronts, send their own confirmation emails and run their own gates. When you buy a ticket or visit an attraction, the organizer decides what information is collected and how it is used, and we process it on the organizer's behalf as its service provider. The organizer's own privacy policy describes its practices, and the organizer is the right first contact for questions about your order. If you contact us instead, we will pass your request to the organizer and help it respond. Guest accounts are kept separate for each organizer: one organizer cannot see your information held by another.

2. Information we collect

On this website

  • Demo requests. If you fill in the demo form, we receive your name, work email, the name of your event or attraction, your city, its approximate size, and anything you write in the notes field. The form is delivered to us by Formspree.
  • Technical information. Our website host, GitHub Pages, records standard request information such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver and secure the site. The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser also connects to Google to fetch them.
  • This website does not use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies.

From organizers and their staff

  • Account information: name, email address, role and the organizations a person belongs to.
  • Sign-in information: one-time sign-in codes, two-step verification settings and passkeys. We keep only what is needed to verify them, never in a readable form.
  • Business information: the organizer's business name, venues, events, prices, policies, branding and settings.
  • Square connection: when an organizer connects its Square account, Square gives us access tokens, which we store encrypted. Payments go straight to the organizer's Square account.
  • Activity records: an audit log of changes made in the admin, for accountability and support.
  • Support messages that organizers send us.

Guest information we process for organizers

  • Contact details: name, email address and, if requested, phone number.
  • Order details: tickets, dates and entry times, prices, fees, discounts, refunds and order history.
  • Answers to questions the organizer asks at checkout, and records of policy or waiver acceptance.
  • Check-in records: when a ticket was scanned, at which entrance, and by which staff member.
  • Messages sent about an order and whether they were delivered, plus marketing preferences.
  • Payment information from Square, limited to what is needed to run the order, such as payment status, card brand and the last four digits. Full card numbers are entered into Square's secure payment form and never reach us.
  • Device and network information, such as IP address and browser type, used to keep checkout secure and to prevent fraud and automated abuse.

3. The TicketBloom Scanner app

The Scanner app is used by an organizer's staff at its entrances and box office. It is not for the general public.

  • Camera. The camera is used only to read ticket QR codes. Images are analyzed on the device and are never stored or uploaded.
  • Device and staff information. When a phone is set up for an organizer, we record the device, its name and app version. Staff sign in with a PIN, which we store only as a one-way hash. While the app is in use it reports its app version, battery level and how many scans are waiting to upload, so the organizer can see that its scanners are working.
  • Check-in records. Each scan records the ticket, the time, the entrance and the staff member, so a ticket cannot be used twice.
  • Offline ticket list. So entrances keep working without a signal, the app stores the current event's ticket list on the phone, encrypted. It includes ticket codes and guest names for lookup. Email addresses and phone numbers are kept only as one-way hashes for searching. The list is removed at the end of a shift or when the organizer removes the device.
  • Notifications. If enabled, the app registers a push notification token so it can be woken to download the ticket list before doors open.
  • The app does not record audio, does not collect location, contacts or photos, does not use an advertising identifier, and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting software.

4. How we use information

  • To provide the service: sell and deliver tickets and wallet passes, process payments and refunds, check guests in, and send the messages organizers set up.
  • To keep TicketBloom secure and reliable: authenticate users, prevent fraud and bot abuse, detect and fix problems, and keep backups.
  • To support organizers and respond to demo requests and questions.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations and to enforce our agreements.
  • To improve TicketBloom, using aggregated or de-identified information that does not identify any guest.

We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising on our own behalf, and we do not use one organizer's guest information for another organizer.

For people in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, our legal bases are performing a contract, our legitimate interests in running and securing the service, consent where it is required, and compliance with the law.

6. Cookies and similar technology

This website sets no cookies of its own. The TicketBloom admin and organizer storefronts use cookies that are needed to keep you signed in, hold tickets in your cart and protect checkout. An organizer may also add analytics or advertising tags to its own storefront; where it does, the storefront asks for consent where the law requires and honors Global Privacy Control signals.

7. How long we keep it

  • Organizer and staff account information: for as long as the account is active, then deleted or de-identified unless we must keep it longer.
  • Guest and order information: for as long as the organizer keeps its account and its retention settings require, so it can handle refunds, disputes, reporting and returning guests. Organizers can ask us to delete guest information.
  • Payment and transaction records: as long as tax and accounting rules require, generally up to seven years.
  • Data exports: deleted automatically seven days after they are created.
  • The Scanner app's offline ticket list: removed at the end of a shift or when the device is removed.
  • Website demo requests: up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you become a customer.
  • Backups: kept for a limited period and then overwritten.

8. How we protect it

We use encryption in transit and at rest, keep each organizer's data separated at the database level, require two-step verification for staff accounts with access to sensitive information, sign tickets so they cannot be forged, limit employee access to what is needed, and monitor the service for misuse. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect information and will notify affected organizers and people as the law requires if a breach occurs.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to get a copy, to correct it, to delete it, and to object to or restrict certain uses. Residents of California and other U.S. states with privacy laws have these rights, as do people in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, who may also complain to their local data protection authority.

  • Ticket buyers and guests: please contact the organizer you bought from first. You can also contact us and we will forward your request and help the organizer respond.
  • Organizers, staff and website visitors: contact us at the address below.
  • Marketing emails: use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.

We will verify your request before acting on it, and you may use an authorized agent where the law allows. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. Because we do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, there is nothing to opt out of on that front.

10. Children

TicketBloom is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from them. Tickets for children are purchased by an adult, who may give a child's name or age where an attraction asks for it. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Where information is stored

TicketBloom stores and processes information in the United States. If you use TicketBloom from another country, your information is transferred to and handled in the United States.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as TicketBloom changes. We will post the new version here with a new date and, for significant changes, notify organizers in advance.
